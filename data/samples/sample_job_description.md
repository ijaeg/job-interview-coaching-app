# Senior Java Backend Developer – Content Platform (m/f/d)

**Elbwerk Media Technology GmbH** · Hamburg, Germany · Hybrid (2 office days per week) · Full-time, permanent

## About us

Elbwerk Media Technology builds the digital platform behind five consumer magazine brands with around 12 million monthly readers. Our 30 engineers work in five cross-functional teams. You will join the five-person Content Platform team, which owns the editorial CMS, the content import and export pipelines and the APIs that deliver articles to our web and app clients. We are in the middle of moving our publishing stack from print-first to digital-first.

## Your responsibilities

- Design, build and operate backend services for content creation, enrichment and delivery with Java and Spring Boot.
- Extend and integrate our enterprise CMS (Magnolia DX Core) with surrounding systems such as DAM, search and the ad server via REST APIs and event streaming.
- Take ownership of the operation, monitoring and stability of the services your team runs in production.
- Make architecture decisions within the team in line with the overall platform architecture.
- Ensure high code quality through code reviews, unit and integration tests and automated CI/CD pipelines.
- Mentor less experienced colleagues and share knowledge actively.
- Use AI tools in your daily work, assess their results critically and help the team adopt them responsibly.

## Your profile

**Required**

- At least 5 years of experience in developing and operating backend software in agile teams.
- Excellent knowledge of Java (17 or later) and Spring Boot.
- Hands-on experience with an enterprise CMS; Magnolia CMS experience is a strong plus.
- Experience with event streaming (Apache Kafka) and REST API design.
- Production experience with container orchestration on **Kubernetes** and infrastructure as code with **Terraform**.
- Experience with monitoring and alerting tools such as Datadog, Prometheus or Grafana.
- Solid knowledge of relational and document databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB).
- Very good English (C1) and good German skills.

**Nice to have**

- Experience with a public cloud, ideally AWS (EKS, S3).
- Frontend experience with Vue.js or React.
- Experience with GraphQL.
